# Shrtn — Investor One-Pager

## The thesis

**Shrtn makes the humble redirect valuable again by selling only what users
actually need: a working link, control over it, or an identity inside it.**

Most link products start simple and become marketing suites. Shrtn goes in the
other direction: it removes dashboards, audience profiling and feature noise.
The product is easy to understand in one sentence:

> Free gives you the redirect. Control gives you control. Brand gives you
> identity.

## The problem

People create short links for one practical reason: a destination needs to be
shared in a compact, reliable form. The moment that link is printed, placed in
a QR code, sent in a campaign, or used by a company, three different needs
emerge:

1. The destination must keep working.
2. It may need to change without replacing the QR code.
3. The URL must look like it belongs to the company using it.

Existing products frequently bundle these needs with analytics, campaigns,
teams and dashboards. That increases complexity for a customer who simply
wants a dependable redirect.

## The product

| Product | Customer value |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | A secure, immutable `shrtn.link` redirect and standard QR code. |
| **Control** | Editable destinations, pause/reactivate and code reservation. |
| **Compact** | `shr.mp` paired compact address for controlled links. |
| **Brand** | A verified URL identity, branded QR, optional Brand Index and branded transition. |

Brand is the economic centre: one subscription provides a verified paired
identity such as `helioh2.shrtn.link` and `hlh2.shr.mp`, while retaining the
ability to create ordinary controlled links on both domains.

## Why this model is defensible

- **Simplicity is a product decision.** No customer-facing analytics means a
  narrower, clearer product and fewer privacy expectations.
- **Paid mutability aligns with abuse control.** Free destinations are fixed;
  editable links require a paying, authenticated account and are rescreened.
- **Brand is scarce by design.** Each identity is reviewed and tied to verified
  company evidence rather than offered as an anonymous vanity field.
- **Printed QR value compounds.** A controlled link keeps the QR code useful
  when the destination changes.
- **Two owned domains create a natural upgrade.** `shrtn.link` is readable;
  `shr.mp` is compact.

## Revenue architecture

| Revenue stream | Placeholder | What it sells |
| --- | ---:| --- |
| Control subscription | €[X]/month | Redirect control |
| Compact add-on | +€[Y]/month | Compact paired URLs |
| Brand subscription | €[Z]/month per brand | Verified identity and branding |
| Unlock editing | €[P] one time per link | Permanent editing without subscription |

The public pricing ladder is intentionally cumulative: **Free → Control →
Brand**. Compact is an add-on to Control and included in Brand.

## Go-to-market and validation

The first goal is not category-scale traffic. It is to validate whether
customers will pay for control and identity without requiring a marketing
platform.

Initial validation channels:

- Gabriel's own companies and QR-heavy use cases.
- Italian SMEs that need a professional, stable link but not a campaign suite.
- Agencies and partner companies that need controlled or branded links for
  clients.

Key evidence to collect:

- Free-to-Control conversion.
- Compact attach rate.
- Brand request-to-approval rate.
- Repeated QR use cases.
- Operational cost per active link and per Brand review.

## Near-term milestones

1. Launch authenticated Free and Control flows.
2. Validate paid editing and Compact demand.
3. Manually approve the first Brands and learn the identity-verification flow.
4. Add future direct sponsorship only after there is meaningful, safe Free
   redirect inventory.

**Shrtn is a focused infrastructure product with a simple revenue ladder: the
customer starts with a redirect and pays only when that redirect becomes an
asset worth controlling or branding.**
