# Shrtn — Sponsorship Partner One-Pager

## A future brand-awareness surface inside an intentional redirect moment

Shrtn is building a simple link-redirection service. Its Free links show a
short, transparent transition page before reaching the intended destination.
At launch, that page is Shrtn-branded only. As qualified traffic grows, Shrtn
will offer a **direct sponsorship programme** for selected Free-link inventory.

This is not programmatic advertising and it is not a click-farming product.
It is a controlled visibility placement for brands that value repeated,
unambiguous exposure.

## The sponsor experience

For a sponsored Free link, the visitor sees:

> We're redirecting you to **[destination]**
> Redirecting in **5 seconds**
> Sponsored by **[Partner]**

The sponsor's approved visual appears as the page background. The central
redirect message remains consistent and the visitor is always sent to the
original destination.

## The rules that protect the placement

- Sponsor artwork is **not clickable**.
- There are no sponsor buttons, pop-ups, QR codes or forced interactions.
- A sponsor cannot alter the intended destination.
- Link creators cannot select, remove or change the sponsor.
- Shrtn assigns sponsorship across eligible Free-link pools.
- Control and Brand links never carry sponsors.
- No programmatic ad network or third-party advertising script is used.

The result is a clean five-second brand impression rather than a deceptive
detour. The user understands where they are going, the creator keeps their
link simple, and the sponsor receives a safe, uncluttered visual environment.

## Why partner with Shrtn

- **High-attention format:** the sponsor is visible during an unavoidable,
  clearly labelled transition moment.
- **Brand-safe mechanics:** no clickbait, no pop-unders, no unrelated landing
  pages and no user-targeted ad technology.
- **Direct relationship:** Shrtn can approve the creative, define the link pool
  and keep the experience consistent.
- **Simple proposition:** awareness, not speculative performance claims.
- **Early-mover access:** partner brands can help shape the initial programme
  and category restrictions.

## What Shrtn will offer when inventory is ready

| Component | Planned approach |
| --- | --- |
| Inventory | Selected pools of eligible Free links |
| Format | Non-clickable background plus sponsor attribution |
| Exposure | Five-second sponsored transition |
| Assignment | Managed by Shrtn, not chosen by link creators |
| Reporting | Minimal delivery statement; detailed reporting remains open |
| Commercial model | To be defined: sponsored URL allocation versus delivered displays |

## Current stage

Shrtn is not selling sponsorship at launch. It first needs to build reliable
Free redirect inventory and understand internal delivery volumes. We are
looking for potential launch partners willing to discuss:

- Brand-safety requirements.
- Suitable creative formats.
- Categories Shrtn should exclude.
- The most credible pricing unit.
- The minimum delivery information a sponsor needs.

**The opportunity is to sponsor a clean, transparent utility—not to interrupt
people with an ad platform.**
